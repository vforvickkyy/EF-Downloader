# Privacy Policy

Eastape Downloader · Version 1.4 · Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information Eastape Downloader ("the Software") collects, why, and how it is handled. The Software is made by Eastape Tech ("we", "us"), who is responsible for this information.

## In short

- To use the Software you register with your name and email address.
- The Software sends us usage statistics so we can keep it working and improve it. They are part of how the Software works and cannot be switched off.
- We never receive the links you paste, the names of your files or folders, or the files themselves.
- We never sell your information.

## What we collect

**1. Installation notice.** When the Software is installed or updated, or when a portable copy starts for the first time, it sends us:

- a random installation ID created by the Software;
- the Software version and, for updates, the previous version;
- your operating system and its version (for example "Windows 11, build 22631" or "macOS 15.1 (Apple silicon)");
- the date and time.

**2. Registration.** Before you can use the Software it asks for your name and email address and for your acceptance of the Terms and Conditions, this Privacy Policy and the Disclaimer. Your details are sent to us with the installation ID, the Software version and your operating system version. If you change them later in Settings, the change is sent to us too.

**3. Usage statistics.** The Software sends us the following, linked to its installation ID (and so to your registration):

- when the Software is opened and closed, and for how long it was open;
- while it is open, every few minutes, whether it is downloading and at what overall speed;
- when you open a link: how many links, whether each is a folder, a file or a Google Docs file, how many files and how much data they hold, how long listing took, and whether it worked (if not, the kind of problem, such as "not shared");
- when a download ends: how many files were downloaded, skipped or failed, how much data, how long it took, the average speed, the speed limit and how many files ran at once, and the kinds of problems that occurred (such as "network" or "disk full");
- which features you use, such as pause, the speed limit or the Google API key (whether one is used, never the key);
- update and announcement activity: which version was offered and installed, and which announcements were shown, clicked or closed.

Usage statistics never include the links you paste, file or folder names, file contents or the location of your download folder.

**4. Support messages.** When you use Contact Support we receive your name, email address, the topic and your message. If you leave "Include technical details" ticked, we also receive:

- the Software and operating system versions and the installation ID;
- your download settings (such as the speed limit and how many files download at once);
- a summary of the current download (numbers of files done, failed and waiting);
- whether a Google API key is set (never the key itself);
- recent error messages, which may contain the name of a file or folder, or the Google Drive link involved.

**5. Update checks.** When the Software starts and every few hours it asks our service whether a newer version or an announcement is available, sending its installation ID and version.

## What we don't collect

- We never see or keep the files you download or their contents.
- We don't collect the links you paste, the names of files or folders, your download history or your browsing activity, except as part of technical details you choose to include in a support message.
- We don't collect your computer's name, your user name on the computer, contacts, location or advertising identifiers.
- The Software contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics.

## Why we use it

- to provide the Software, keep a record of installations and registered users, and offer updates;
- to find and fix problems, and to decide what to improve, based on how the Software is used;
- to answer your support requests;
- to contact you about important matters concerning the Software, such as required updates or security issues.

We process this information because it is needed to provide the Software under the Terms you accept, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the Software working, secure and useful. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their marketing.

## How information is sent and stored

Information is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to services we operate:

- the Eastape Downloader service, which stores it in a database. It is hosted for us by Vercel (servers) and Neon (database), in Singapore;
- for installation notices, registrations and support messages, also a service on Google Apps Script, which delivers them to us by email and may record them in a spreadsheet we keep.

These providers process the information only to run these services for us. If your computer is offline, the Software keeps the information on your computer and sends it the next time it can.

## Information stored on your computer

Your settings, registration details, recent links, unsent messages and statistics, and your optional Google API key are stored only on your computer, in %APPDATA%\EastapeDownloader. Downloaded updates are kept briefly in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EastapeDownloader. When you uninstall the Software it offers to delete your settings.

## Connections to Google

To list and download files, the Software connects directly to Google's servers (drive.google.com, drive.usercontent.google.com, docs.google.com, and www.googleapis.com when an API key is used). Google's own privacy policy applies to those connections.

## How long we keep it

We keep registration and installation information while you use the Software and for a reasonable time afterwards, and support messages for as long as needed to help you. Detailed usage statistics are deleted after 180 days; only daily totals for all users together (such as "files downloaded that day") are kept longer.

## Your choices and rights

Usage statistics are required to use the Software. If you don't want them sent, stop using the Software and uninstall it; nothing more is sent after that. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you through Contact Support. Depending on where you live, local data-protection law may give you further rights.

## Children

The Software is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the app under Settings → Legal. When we make important changes, the Software asks you to accept them before you continue.

## Contact

Use Settings → Help & Support → Contact support in the app.
